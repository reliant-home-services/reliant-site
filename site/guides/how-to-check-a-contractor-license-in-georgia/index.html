How to Check a Contractor's License in Georgia

Before you hire anyone for a remodel or repair, take two minutes to confirm they are licensed. In Georgia it is free and easy to check.

When Georgia requires a contractor license

Georgia generally requires a state contractor license for residential construction, remodeling and repair work valued at more than $2,500. Licenses are issued by the State Licensing Board for Residential and General Contractors, which is part of the Georgia Secretary of State's office.

Some trades, including electrical, plumbing and heating and air, are licensed separately. Cities and counties may also require a local business license.

How to look up a license

  • Ask the contractor for their license number and the exact name on the license.
  • Go to the Secretary of State's license lookup at verify.sos.ga.gov.
  • Search by license number or by name.
  • Check that the license is active, that the name matches the company you are hiring, and that the license type fits your project.

What else to ask for

  • Proof of insurance. Ask for a certificate of insurance showing general liability coverage.
  • A written estimate. It should list the work, the materials and the price, with no vague lines.
  • A clear schedule. You should know when work starts and roughly when it ends.
  • Who will be on the job. Ask whether the person quoting the work is the person doing it.

Warning signs

  • No license number, or a number that does not match the company name
  • Pressure to sign today
  • A large cash payment demanded up front
  • No written estimate or contract
  • A price far below every other estimate
