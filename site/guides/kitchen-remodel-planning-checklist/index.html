Kitchen Remodel Planning Checklist

A kitchen remodel goes smoothly when the decisions are made before construction starts. Use this checklist to get ready.

1. Decide what you want to fix

Write down what does not work in your kitchen now: not enough counter space, poor lighting, a cramped layout, worn cabinets. A clear list keeps the project focused and helps your contractor give you an accurate estimate.

2. Set a budget and a priority list

Cabinets and countertops usually take the largest share of a kitchen budget, followed by labor, flooring and appliances. Decide which items matter most to you so you know where to spend and where to save. Keep some money in reserve for surprises behind the walls, especially in an older home.

3. Decide whether the layout changes

Keeping the sink, range and walls where they are is the most economical route. Moving plumbing, gas lines or walls opens up more options but adds cost and time. If you want an island or an open plan, raise it at the first meeting so it can be planned properly.

4. Choose materials early

  • Cabinet style, finish and hardware
  • Countertop material
  • Backsplash tile
  • Flooring
  • Sink, faucet and lighting
  • Appliances, with their exact sizes

Cabinets and countertops often have the longest wait, so ordering them early keeps the job from stalling.

5. Ask about permits

Cosmetic updates often do not need a permit, but structural, electrical and plumbing changes usually do. Requirements vary by city and county in North Georgia. A licensed contractor should handle the permits and inspections for you.

6. Plan for life without a kitchen

Set up a temporary kitchen with a microwave, coffee maker and a small refrigerator in another room. Ask your contractor which days water and power to the kitchen will be off, and how the work area will be kept separate from the rest of the house.

7. Get a detailed written estimate

A good estimate lists what is included, the materials, and the schedule. Compare estimates line by line, not just by the total. Reliant Consulting and Construction provides free, detailed estimates with no hidden costs, and Mark or Dylan walks you through every line.
