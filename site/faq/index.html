Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about working with Reliant Consulting and Construction.

What types of projects does Reliant Consulting and Construction handle?

Reliant Consulting and Construction provides full remodeling, renovation, and new construction services for both residential and commercial properties. Whether you are updating a kitchen or bathroom, expanding your space, or building a new home or office, our team delivers quality work from start to finish.

How long does a remodel or renovation take?

Project timelines vary based on size and scope, but many bathroom remodels are completed in about four weeks. Larger renovations and new builds may take longer. Mark and Dylan provide a clear schedule before work begins so you always know what to expect.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Reliant Consulting and Construction provides free, detailed estimates for all remodeling and construction projects. We take the time to understand your goals, explain your options, and outline costs upfront so there are no surprises later.

What areas do you serve?

Reliant Consulting and Construction proudly serves Jasper, Ball Ground, Marietta, Alpharetta, and surrounding North Georgia communities. If you are unsure whether your area is covered, contact our team and we will be happy to help.

Still have a question?

Call or send us a message. Mark and Dylan are happy to help.
