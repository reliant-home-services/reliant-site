Water Damage Restoration in Jasper and North Georgia

Fast response to burst pipes, leaks and flooding: water removal, drying, repair and full restoration.

Water Damage Restoration by a local, family-owned contractor

Water damage gets worse by the hour. Wet drywall, insulation and flooring hold moisture, and mold can follow if they are not dried or removed. Reliant Consulting and Construction responds to water damage in Jasper and across North Georgia.

We extract the water, dry the structure, remove what cannot be saved and rebuild: framing, subfloor, drywall, flooring, cabinets and trim. One team handles it from the first visit to the finished repair.

We document the damage for your insurance company and work with your adjuster directly.

What to do when you find water damage

If it is safe, shut off the water at the main valve and turn off power to the affected area. Move furniture and valuables out of the water, take photos, and call your insurance company.

Then call us. Homeowners insurance often covers sudden damage such as a burst pipe, while slow leaks and flooding from outside are treated differently, so check your policy or ask your agent. We tell you plainly what we find and what it takes to fix.

Water Damage Restoration FAQs

How quickly should water damage be handled?

As quickly as possible. The longer materials stay wet, the more has to be replaced and the higher the chance of mold. Call as soon as you find the problem.

Do you work with insurance on water damage claims?

Yes. We document the damage and work directly with your insurance company and adjuster.

Do you repair the damage or only dry it out?

Both. We remove the water and dry the structure, then rebuild what was damaged, including subfloor, drywall, flooring and cabinets.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage?

It depends on the cause and your policy. Sudden events like a burst pipe are often covered, while gradual leaks and outside flooding may not be. Your agent can confirm what applies to you.
