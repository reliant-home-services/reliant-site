Kitchen Remodeling by a local, family-owned contractor

The kitchen is the room most homeowners want to change first, and the one where the details matter most. Reliant Consulting and Construction remodels kitchens across Jasper, Pickens County and North Georgia, from a cabinet and countertop refresh to a full tear-out with a new layout.

Mark and Dylan handle the whole job: demolition, framing changes, drywall, cabinets, countertops, tile, flooring, trim and paint. When a kitchen needs plumbing or electrical moved, we plan that work into the schedule so the project keeps moving.

What affects the cost and timeline of a kitchen remodel

The biggest factors are the size of the kitchen, whether the layout changes, and the materials you choose. Keeping sinks, ranges and walls where they are costs less than moving them. Cabinets and countertops are usually the largest share of the budget.

Timelines depend on scope and on how long cabinets and countertops take to arrive. We give you a schedule before work begins, order long-lead materials early, and tell you up front which days the kitchen will be out of use.

Kitchen Remodeling FAQs

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

It depends on the scope. A cabinet and countertop update is much shorter than a full tear-out with layout changes. We give you a clear schedule with your estimate so you know what to expect before work begins.

Can you change the layout of my kitchen?

Yes. We remove and move walls, add islands and rework the layout. Structural changes are planned and built to code, and we handle the permits the work requires.

Do you offer free kitchen remodeling estimates?

Yes. Mark or Dylan visits your home, measures the kitchen, talks through your options and provides a free, detailed estimate with no hidden costs.

Do I need to move out during a kitchen remodel?

Most homeowners stay in the home. We keep the work area contained, clean up as we go, and tell you ahead of time when water or power to the kitchen will be off.
