Bathroom Remodeling in Jasper and North Georgia

Walk-in showers, tile, vanities and full bathroom renovations, with many projects finished in about four weeks.

Bathroom Remodeling by a local, family-owned contractor

A bathroom remodel is one of the most useful upgrades you can make to a home. Reliant Consulting and Construction remodels bathrooms in Jasper and across North Georgia, from a new vanity and tile to a complete gut and rebuild.

We build walk-in showers with tile walls and built-in niches, replace tubs, install vanities and countertops, lay tile and wood-look flooring, and update lighting and fixtures. Behind the walls we replace damaged framing and subfloor so the new finishes last.

Mark and Dylan do the work themselves and keep the job site clean, which matters when the room being remodeled is one your family uses every day.

What affects the cost and timeline of a bathroom remodel

Size, tile choices and plumbing changes drive the budget. Keeping the toilet, shower and vanity in the same positions is the most economical route. Moving drains, enlarging the shower or adding custom tile work adds labor.

Many of our bathroom remodels are completed in about four weeks. Larger primary bathrooms or projects with layout changes can take longer. You get the schedule in writing before we start.

Bathroom Remodeling FAQs

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

Many bathroom remodels are completed in about four weeks. The exact time depends on the size of the room, the tile work and whether plumbing is moved. We provide a schedule before work begins.

Can you convert a tub to a walk-in shower?

Yes. Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common bathroom projects, including tile walls, built-in niches and glass doors.

Do you repair water damage found during a bathroom remodel?

Yes. If we open a wall or floor and find rot or water damage, we show you, explain the fix and repair the framing or subfloor before new finishes go in.

Do you offer free bathroom remodeling estimates?

Yes. We provide free, detailed estimates and explain your options and costs upfront.
