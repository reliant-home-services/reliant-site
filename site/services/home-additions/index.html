Home Additions in Jasper and North Georgia

Room additions, garages and expanded living space, built to match your existing home from foundation to roof.

Home Additions by a local, family-owned contractor

When you like where you live but need more room, an addition is often a better answer than moving. Reliant Consulting and Construction builds home additions in Jasper and across North Georgia.

We manage every phase: foundation, framing, roofing, siding, drywall and interior finishes. The goal is an addition that looks like it was always part of the house, with rooflines, siding and trim that match.

Mark and Dylan bring over 30 years of combined experience in framing and structural work, and they manage permits and inspections from start to final sign-off.

What affects the cost and timeline of a home addition

Size, foundation type and what goes inside the new space are the main factors. A bedroom costs less per square foot than a bathroom or kitchen addition because it has less plumbing and fewer fixtures. Sloped lots can add foundation work.

An addition involves permits, inspections and weather, so we lay out the schedule phase by phase before we break ground and keep you updated as each phase is completed.

Home Additions FAQs

Do you handle permits for home additions?

Yes. We manage the permits and inspections for the addition, from the first application to the final inspection.

Will the addition match my existing house?

That is the goal on every project. We match rooflines, siding, trim and interior finishes so the new space blends with the original home.

Can you build a garage?

Yes. We build attached and detached garages, handling every part of the project from the foundation to the roof.

Do you offer free estimates for additions?

Yes. We meet with you at your home, look at the site and provide a free, detailed estimate.
