Decks, Patios and Outdoor Living in North Georgia

Custom decks, patios, porches, pavilions, retaining walls and concrete work that extend your living space outdoors.

Decks and Patios by a local, family-owned contractor

North Georgia weather makes outdoor space usable most of the year. Reliant Consulting and Construction builds decks, patios, porches and pavilions for homeowners in Jasper and the surrounding communities.

Many lots here are sloped, so outdoor projects often start with the ground: retaining walls, terracing and proper footings. We build the structure to code with quality materials so it holds up for years.

We also pour concrete slabs, pads and driveways, and replace worn-out driveways and patios.

What affects the cost of a deck or patio

Size, height off the ground and materials are the main factors. A ground-level patio is simpler than a raised deck with stairs and railings. Covered structures such as porches and pavilions add framing and roofing.

Slope matters too. A steep yard may need retaining walls or taller posts and deeper footings. We look at the site with you and explain the options before you commit to a design.

Decks and Patios FAQs

Do you build covered porches and pavilions?

Yes. We build covered porches, pavilions and gazebos, including the framing, roofing and finish details.

Can you build on a sloped yard?

Yes. Sloped lots are common in North Georgia. We build retaining walls and terraces and set proper footings so decks and patios are solid on uneven ground.

Do you pour driveways?

Yes. We pour concrete driveways, slabs and pads and install asphalt driveways, including replacing old, damaged ones.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We visit the site, talk through your ideas and provide a free, detailed estimate.
