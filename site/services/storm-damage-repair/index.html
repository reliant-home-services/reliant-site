What to do after a storm

Stay clear of downed power lines and damaged parts of the structure. From the ground, photograph the roof, siding and any fallen trees, then report the damage to your insurance company.

Call us to inspect it. Storm damage is not always visible from the yard, and a damaged roof can leak for weeks before a stain shows on the ceiling. We explain what we find and give you and your insurer a clear scope of the repair.

Storm Damage Repair FAQs

Do you help with storm damage insurance claims?

Yes. We document the damage and support your claim, working with your insurance company and adjuster.

Do you repair roofs after a storm?

Yes. We repair and replace storm-damaged roofs and fix any structural or interior damage that came with it.

A tree fell on my house. Can you fix it?

Yes. We repair structural damage from fallen trees and limbs, including framing, roofing and the interior.

How do I know if my roof has storm damage?

Missing shingles, dented metal and granules in the gutters are common signs, but some damage cannot be seen from the ground. An inspection is the reliable way to know.
