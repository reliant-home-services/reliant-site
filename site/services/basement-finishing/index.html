Basement Finishing in Jasper and North Georgia

Turn an unfinished basement into living space: family rooms, bedrooms, bathrooms, offices and media rooms.

Basement Finishing by a local, family-owned contractor

Finishing a basement is usually the least expensive way to add living space, because the foundation, walls and roof are already there. Reliant Consulting and Construction finishes basements for homeowners in Jasper and across North Georgia.

We frame the walls, insulate, hang and finish drywall, install flooring and trim, and build the rooms you need: a family room, guest bedroom, bathroom, office, gym or media room. We handle the permits and coordinate the inspections the work requires.

Many North Georgia homes sit on sloped lots with walk-out basements, which makes them good candidates for bright, comfortable finished space.

What affects the cost and timeline of finishing a basement

The main factors are square footage, how many rooms you divide the space into, and whether you add a bathroom or wet bar. Plumbing below grade and extra egress windows add to the scope.

Before we frame anything we look for moisture. A basement needs to be dry before it is finished, so if we see signs of water we tell you what should be addressed first.

Basement Finishing FAQs

How long does it take to finish a basement?

It depends on the size of the basement and what goes into it. An open family room is quicker than a basement with a bedroom, bathroom and media room. We give you a schedule with your estimate.

Do I need a permit to finish my basement?

Finishing a basement usually requires a permit because it involves framing, electrical and often plumbing. We handle the permits and inspections as part of the job.

Can you add a bathroom in a basement?

Yes. We build basement bathrooms, including the framing, tile and fixtures, and coordinate the plumbing work it requires.

What if my basement has moisture?

We check for moisture before work starts. If there is a problem, we explain what needs to be corrected before the space is finished so your new walls and floors are protected.
