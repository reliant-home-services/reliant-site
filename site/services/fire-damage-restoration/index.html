Fire Damage Restoration in Jasper and North Georgia

Smoke and soot removal, structural repair and full reconstruction, with help on the insurance claim.

Fire Damage Restoration by a local, family-owned contractor

A house fire is overwhelming, and the damage goes beyond what burned. Smoke, soot and the water used to put the fire out all affect the structure. Reliant Consulting and Construction restores fire-damaged homes in Jasper and across North Georgia.

We assess the structure, remove damaged materials, clean smoke and soot, deal with odor, and rebuild. Because we are general contractors, the same team that tears out the damage frames, drywalls and finishes the repair. You are not handed from one company to another.

We work directly with your insurance company and document the damage so the claim is as simple as it can be.

What to do after a fire

Once the fire department says it is safe, call your insurance company to start a claim, and avoid cleaning soot yourself, since wiping it can set stains deeper. Take photos if you can do so safely.

Then call us. We inspect the damage, explain what can be cleaned and what must be replaced, and give your insurer the documentation they ask for. Most fire restoration is paid through homeowners insurance, and what is covered depends on your policy.

Fire Damage Restoration FAQs

Do you work with insurance companies on fire damage?

Yes. We work directly with your insurance company and provide the documentation needed for the claim.

Can you rebuild after a major fire?

Yes. We handle everything from smoke and soot removal to full reconstruction, including framing, roofing, drywall and finishes.

Can smoke odor be removed?

In most cases, yes. Odor removal is part of our fire restoration work, along with removing materials that cannot be cleaned.

How soon should I call after a fire?

Call as soon as the property is safe to enter. The sooner the damage is assessed and the home is secured, the less secondary damage there is from soot and water.
