About Reliant Consulting and Construction

30+ years of combined experience. Father and son, Mark and Dylan.

Our Story

Reliant Consulting and Construction is a family-owned and operated company led by father and son, Mark and Dylan. With more than thirty years of combined experience, they bring true craftsmanship, care, and attention to detail to every project they touch. What began as Mark's vision has grown into a partnership built on doing things the right way the first time.

They are not a large corporate contractor. They are a small, hands-on team that takes the time to understand your goals, answer your questions, and make sure everything is completed with excellence. When you hire Reliant, you get Mark and Dylan on your job site - not a rotation of subcontractors you have never met.

From interior remodels and complete renovations to insurance restoration work, Reliant Consulting and Construction has helped families across North Georgia update, rebuild, and restore their homes. Their reputation is built on honesty, reliability, and lasting quality.

For Mark and Dylan, building is more than work. It is their passion and their family name on every project. They don't just transform spaces - they build trust, one home at a time.
