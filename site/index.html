Frequently Asked Questions About Our Services in Jasper

When it comes to your home or business, we know how important it is to feel confident in who you hire. That is why our team is always ready to answer your questions and explain every step of the process.

What types of projects does Reliant Home Services handle?

Reliant Home Services provides full remodeling, renovation, and new construction services for both residential and commercial properties. Whether you are updating a kitchen or bathroom, expanding your space, or building a new home or office, our team delivers quality work from start to finish.

How long does a remodel or renovation take?

Project timelines vary based on size and scope, but many bathroom remodels are completed in about four weeks. Larger renovations and new builds may take longer. Mark and Dylan provide a clear schedule before work begins so you always know what to expect.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Reliant Home Services provides free, detailed estimates for all remodeling and construction projects. We take the time to understand your goals, explain your options, and outline costs upfront so there are no surprises later.

What areas do you serve?

Reliant Home Services proudly serves Jasper, Ball Ground, Marietta, Alpharetta, and surrounding North Georgia communities. If you are unsure whether your area is covered, contact our team and we will be happy to help.
